Before your first Thursday

Good to know.

Who can come?+

Everyone is welcome. This is an open community gathering — come solo or bring a friend.

What should I bring?+

Bring a swimsuit, towel, water, sandals and something warm to wear after your final cold plunge.

Do I need to reserve?+

Advance payment is the best way to secure your spot. Use the Reserve & Pay button, then contact us on WhatsApp if you have any questions.

What is included?+

The experience includes the wood-fired Sauna on Wheels, cold plunge, cold shower, tea ceremony and a relaxed community atmosphere.

Where do I park?+

The gathering is at SC Fight House, 2740 Camino Capistrano in San Clemente. Open the directions link for the exact destination.
