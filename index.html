Before you book

Good to know.

How many people fit?+

The sauna is designed for a comfortable shared session. Final capacity and recommended group size will be confirmed with your booking.

Where can you deliver?+

Our core area is coastal Los Angeles and Orange County, with winter destination service planned for Mammoth Lakes and Lake Tahoe.

How long does setup take?+

We deliver, position, heat, and prepare the sauna before your reserved session so your only job is to step inside.

What should I bring?+

A towel, water, sandals, and something warm for the cool-down. We provide the heat, atmosphere, and sauna essentials.
