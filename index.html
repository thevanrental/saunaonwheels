How It Works

We deliver a fully heated wood-fired sauna to your driveway, backyard, private event, or approved event location.

1. We coordinate the location and access
2. We deliver and set up the sauna
3. The sauna arrives heated and ready to use
4. You enjoy your private or event sauna session
5. We pick it up after the session or event

Includes:
• Fully heated wood-fired sauna
• Firewood
• Setup & pickup
• Optional guided sauna experience
