Before you book

Good to know.

How many people fit?+

The sauna seats up to 12 people at a time. For larger events, share your total guest count so we can plan sessions and discuss the setup.

How much does mobile sauna rental cost?+

Orange County private sauna packages are $450 for 3 hours or $600 for a full day. Los Angeles, mountain destinations and custom events are quoted separately.

What is included in an Orange County private sauna booking?+

The $450 three-hour and $600 full-day packages include Orange County delivery and pickup, full setup, heating, firewood and two venik bundles. Cold plunge and other extras are separate.

Can I rent a cold plunge without the sauna?+

Yes. Standalone cold-plunge rentals start at $250 per plunge in Orange County. Delivery, ice, on-site service, timing and additional requirements are confirmed by request.

Where can you deliver?+

We deliver across Orange County and Los Angeles. Winter delivery to Mammoth Lakes and Lake Tahoe is available by custom request, subject to availability, weather, road conditions and access at your location.

Can you bring a sauna to a public beach?+

Tell us the beach and exact location before booking. Delivery depends on trailer access, suitable placement and permission from the property owner or site operator. A requested beach location is confirmed individually.

When is the sauna ready?+

We arrange delivery, positioning, heating and setup around your reserved session. Timing depends on the location and is confirmed when you book.

What should I bring?+

Bring a swimsuit, towel, drinking water, sandals and something warm for the cool-down.

Do you provide sauna rentals for events and film shoots?+

Yes. Request a custom quote for private parties, corporate wellness, retreats, film and TV, commercials, photo shoots or music videos. Tell us your location, date, guest or crew count and whether you need cold plunge or staff.
